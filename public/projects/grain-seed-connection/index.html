Grain Seed Connection

June 10, 2026 · 2 min read

The Grain Seed Connection is the thread between the Grainworks philosophy and the physical world: automatic plant watering systems that respect the natural rhythm of the soil. No pumps, no timers, no electricity — just fired clay, water, and capillary action.

At the center of it: Blumat, an Austrian company that has been manufacturing ceramic clay cone watering systems since 1965.

The Clay Cone

The heart of every Blumat product is a hollow-cast clay cone, fired at over 1000°C. Buried in the soil, it acts as both sensor and actuator:

  1. Dry soil → the porous clay releases water via capillary action
  2. Wet soil → water flow stops naturally
  3. Each plant is independent — every cone regulates its own watering

No electricity. No batteries. No programming. Just physics.

Why the “Grain Seed Connection”

The name connects two ideas:

  • Grain — the direction things want to go. Wood has grain, circuits have grain, software has grain. Soil has grain too — the natural lines along which water flows, roots spread, and plants grow.
  • Seed — the starting point. A watering system isn’t the end goal; it’s the seed of a larger idea: tools that work with natural systems instead of overriding them.

Blumat works with the grain of the soil. It doesn’t force water on a schedule — it responds to the plant’s actual need. That’s the connection.

Product Research

Tropf-Blumat (Drip System — Flagship)

The core system: clay cone + silicone tube to water source. When soil dries, it drips. When soil is wet, it stops.
