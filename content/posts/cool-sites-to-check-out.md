A list I keep adding to. Each one is worth at least a half-hour of clicking around.

**[Wokwi.com](https://wokwi.com)** — In-browser electronics simulator for Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi Pico. Write code, wire up components, watch it run in real time. No hardware needed, no setup. The simulator handles I2C, SPI, UART, and the built-in logic analyzer catches timing bugs before you burn a chip. Essential for prototyping IoT projects when your desk is already four feet deep in other prototypes.

**[Geo-fs.com](https://geo-fs.com)** — Vector-based world map with terrain, roads, and satellite imagery, rendered in the browser at remarkable detail. Serves as a lightweight alternative to Google Earth for quick geographic exploration. Good for route planning, understanding terrain, or just flying around the planet when you need a mental reset.

**[Cleanpng.com](https://cleanpng.com)** — Massive library of transparent PNG assets. No background, no watermark on the useful ones. Searchable by keyword, organized by category. Useful for mockups, presentations, collages, or any project where you need a clean cutout without firing up Photoshop.

**[Emergent.sh](https://emergent.sh)** — Infrastructure-as-code platform with a focus on simplicity. Provision and manage cloud infrastructure through declarative config files. Appeals to the same crowd that appreciates Terraform but wants something lighter, more opinionated, less mired in HCL verbosity.

**[Blueprint.am](https://blueprint.am)** — Collaborative design and prototyping tool. Visual canvas for mapping out systems, workflows, architectures. Good for whiteboarding sessions that need to persist as documented artifacts rather than disappearing into the void of a video call.

**[Wokwi.com](https://wokwi.com)** — Deserved a second mention because it's genuinely that useful. If you build anything with microcontrollers, bookmark it.

**[WolframAlpha.com](https://wolframalpha.com)** — Computational knowledge engine. Type a query, get an answer backed by curated data and symbolic computation. Unit conversions, integrals, chemical properties, historical dates, nutritional data, natural language math. Works when Google gives you a link farm and you just want the number.

**[Lunchboxsessions.com](https://lunchboxsessions.com)** — Browser-based 3D sessions and collaborative environments. Stream lightweight 3D scenes for design reviews, spatial planning, or just messing around with geometry in real time with other people.
