**Status: Research complete. Briefing compiled.**

A comprehensive deep-dive into the Josh.ai voice control platform — architecture, dealer onboarding, competitive positioning, and integration capabilities for the ELV (Electronic Lifestyles) market.

## Research Scope

- **Platform architecture:** Josh Core, Edge, Nano, Micro — what each processor does and how they differ
- **Integration ecosystem:** Lutron, Sonos, AV systems, lighting, shading, HVAC, pools, security, and 50+ other integrations
- **Dealer training:** Onboarding process, certification requirements, programming tools
- **Competitive analysis:** Comparison with Control4, Crestron, Savant, and DIY alternatives
- **Market positioning:** Where Josh.ai fits in the ELV landscape

## Key Findings

- Josh.ai's strength is ease of programming and natural language voice control
- The nano processor is the entry point; most dealers start with Core for whole-home
- Lutron integration is the deepest and most mature
- The platform is rapidly expanding its integration library (50+ partners as of 2026)
