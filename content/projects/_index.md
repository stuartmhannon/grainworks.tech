## What is this?

A single place to see everything we're working on — the tiny robot rolling around on ESP32 wheels, the Blumat watering system that works with the grain of the soil, the split-flap display that turns any TV into an airport departure board, and the agent-persona library that's reshaping how we think about AI identity.

Each project page has: what it is, the files associated with it, its current build/research status, the raw research that went into it, and a roadmap for what's next.

This is a living document — projects move from planning → research → prototype → shipped as we work through them.

## How to navigate

- **Hardware projects** (robot, watering system) — schematics, BOMs, build notes
- **Software projects** (FlipOff, SOUL.md) — repos, architecture, deployment
- **Research** (memory systems, Blumat) — raw investigation, comparison tables, sources
- **Grain Tracker** — the meta-view: what's active, blocked, or shipping next
