**Status: Research compiled.**

A technical deep-dive into Blumat automatic watering systems — the pressure-regulated drip irrigation systems that use ceramic soil moisture sensors instead of electronic timers. No power, no pumps, no controllers — just physics.

## Key Areas

- **System architecture:** How the ceramic cone sensors regulate water flow based on soil tension
- **Setup considerations:** Pressure regulation, line runs, emitter placement, system bleeding
- **Integration potential:** Adding IoT monitoring (moisture sensors, flow meters) to the purely mechanical system
- **Scale considerations:** Small garden vs greenhouse vs market garden configurations
